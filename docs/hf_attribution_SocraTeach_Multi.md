# Attribution — SocraTeach_Multi

## Original Work

This dataset was produced by the SocraticLM research team at USTC (CogBase lab):

> Liu, Jiayu and Huang, Zhenya and Xiao, Tong and Sha, Jing and Wu, Jinze and Liu, Qi and Wang, Shijin and Chen, Enhong.
> "SocraticLM: Exploring Socratic Personalized Teaching with Large Language Models."
> *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight)*, vol. 37, pp. 85693–85721.
> https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/9bae399d1f34b8650351c1bd3692aeae-Paper-Conference.pdf

All dataset content, the SocraTeach construction methodology, and the six student persona framework are the intellectual property of the SocraticLM research team.

## Source Data

The math problems in this dataset are drawn from:
- **GSM8K** — Cobbe et al., 2021 (grade school math word problems)
- **MAWPS** — Math word problem benchmark

The original SocraTeach dataset is distributed via:
- GitHub: https://github.com/Ljyustc/SocraticLM

The source data was NOT published directly on HuggingFace by the original authors.

## HuggingFace Upload

This HuggingFace upload was created by **Ulises Chavarria** to make the dataset more accessible for the research community.

- Uploader: Ulises Chavarria
- Context: CSEN 346 (Natural Language Processing), Santa Clara University
